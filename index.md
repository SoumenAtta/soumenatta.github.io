## About

Dr. Soumen Atta holds a Ph.D. (Engg.), M.Tech. (C.S.E.), B.Tech. (I.T.), and has qualified UGC NET, W.B. SET, and GATE. He has also earned a **Habilitation in Computer Science (Docent)**.

He is currently a **<a href="https://www.jyu.fi/en/people/soumen-atta" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Postdoctoral Researcher (Career Stage II)</a>** at the Faculty of Information Technology, University of Jyväskylä, Finland, and a Visiting Researcher at Iscte – Lisbon and Leiden University. He has over four years of postdoctoral research experience across the Czech Republic, France, and Finland, and over three years of experience as an Assistant Professor in Slovenia and India.
